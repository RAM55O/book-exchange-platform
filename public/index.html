How BookSwap Works

Simple 3-step process to exchange your books without spending money

1. Post a Book

Fill out book name, author, condition, and language. It's listed for others instantly.

2. Discover & Request

Browse the library, filter by language or condition, and send a swap request with an exchange offer.

3. Owner Accepts & Swap

The owner reviews the incoming request, accepts the offer, and you connect to swap books!
